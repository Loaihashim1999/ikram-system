## Association frame

The frame is a 0.6mm green line. On the receipt it starts just below the letterhead, encloses the title, table, note, and signature lines, and stops above the page number and footer artwork. It does not cross the table. Measured bounds at 110 DPI: about 10mm from the left edge and about 56mm to 261mm down the page.

## Daily report pagination

Page 1 is filled by the KPI row and the two operational tables, down to the footer. The signature block does not fit in the remaining space without overlapping the footer. It stays together on page 2. That second page is sparse, and it is required by the content already on page 1. Spacing was reduced; the page was not forced back onto page 1.

## Governance comparison

Page 7 shows dark green bars next to the counts. The longer bar matches the larger count. The number remains in its own column. Header, table, page number, and footer stay separated.

## Other checks

Arabic text is RTL. Mixed Arabic and English in the receipt wraps inside the cell. Page numbers are visible, including page 31 of 31 on the earlier governance render. No blank page was found in the inspected set.
